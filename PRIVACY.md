# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 4, 2026

## Overview

MultiSearch is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains our data practices.

## Data Collection

**We DO NOT collect any data.**

Specifically:
- ❌ No browsing history
- ❌ No search queries
- ❌ No personal information
- ❌ No usage analytics
- ❌ No tracking cookies
- ❌ No telemetry

## How the Extension Works

MultiSearch operates **entirely locally** in your browser:

1. **Detects** which search engine you're on (by reading the page URL)
2. **Reads** your search query from the search input field
3. **Creates** buttons linking to other search engines
4. **No data** is sent to external servers

## Permissions

The extension requires one permission:

- **`activeTab`**: Allows the extension to read the current page URL and search query to create the switching buttons. This permission is limited to the active tab only and cannot access other tabs or your browsing history.

## Third-Party Services

The extension does **not** use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

When you click a button to switch search engines, you are redirected to that search engine's website. Each search engine has its own privacy policy:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Bing Privacy Policy](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement)
- [DuckDuckGo Privacy Policy](https://duckduckgo.com/privacy)
- [Ecosia Privacy Policy](https://www.ecosia.org/privacy)
- [Brave Privacy Policy](https://brave.com/privacy/)
- [Startpage Privacy Policy](https://www.startpage.com/privacy/)
- [Qwant Privacy Policy](https://help.qwant.com/privacy/)
- [Mojeek Privacy Policy](https://www.mojeek.com/about/privacy/)
